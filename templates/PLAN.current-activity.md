## Completion Checklist

Review-specific checklist items apply when the relevant review is required. If a
review is not required, record the skip reason in the matching review section. If
required review is unavailable or forbidden, record a blocker.

- [ ] Local `docs/GOAL.md` reviewed for alignment.
- [ ] Related files read.
- [ ] Required references cached as safe, approved local notes under
      `docs/references/<topic>/<reference>.md`.
- [ ] Pre-implementation review completed or not-required skip documented.
- [ ] Pre-implementation findings validated by the primary assistant, resolved,
      and reviewers closed.
- [ ] No unresolved pre-implementation findings, gaps, or blockers remain.
- [ ] Tests written or updated first where practical, or skipped with reason.
- [ ] Implementation completed within scope.
- [ ] Targeted validation run.
- [ ] Full validation gate run or skipped with reason.
- [ ] Post-change review completed or not-required skip documented.
- [ ] Post-change findings validated by the primary assistant, resolved, and
      reviewers closed.
- [ ] No unresolved post-change findings, gaps, or blockers remain.
- [ ] Full diff reviewed.
- [ ] Local-only files, `/docs` contents, and secrets excluded unless explicitly
      approved for tracking.
- [ ] Handoff includes validation and residual risk.
